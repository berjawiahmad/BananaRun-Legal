1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how LebGames (“we”, “us”, or the “Service Provider”) processes information in Banana Run (the “Application”), a free-to-play mobile game for a mixed-age audience, including children. Contact: support.lebgames@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features you use, we and our service providers may process: an anonymous Unity Authentication player identifier; an optional linked Google Play Games account identifier; nickname and profile settings; game progress, scores, virtual items and achievements; cloud-saved settings; friend and leaderboard information; custom levels; profile pictures; support communications; and technical information such as app version, device type, operating system, IP address, diagnostics and service logs. Advertising services may process advertising or device identifiers where permitted and configured. Some information is supplied by you; other information is generated when you use the Application or its integrated services. We do not claim that all third-party data is anonymous.

3. Purposes of processing

We process information to operate gameplay and accounts, save progress, provide selected online and social features, moderate submitted content, deliver optional rewarded ads, detect abuse and technical problems, respond to support or deletion requests, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use the Application to request precise device location for location-based gameplay; an IP address may nonetheless indicate an approximate region to service providers.

4. Age screening and children

Banana Run includes an age screen to help determine an age-appropriate experience and advertising configuration. Information entered for age screening should be used only as necessary for that purpose. Children's personal information is subject to additional legal protections. We do not knowingly use children's personal information for behavioral advertising where prohibited. Any feature that requires parental consent under applicable law must not process the child's information for that purpose without the required consent. Parents or guardians may contact support.lebgames@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning their children. Availability of public profiles, user-generated content, and social features must follow the safety and privacy controls implemented for the relevant age group.

5. Optional rewarded advertisements

Banana Run uses Unity Ads for optional rewarded videos that may provide coins, lives, or other in-game rewards. Ads are not required for ordinary gameplay. Unity and its advertising partners may process device, network, ad delivery and interaction information according to the SDK configuration, user age and applicable law. Age-related settings are used to help restrict advertising behavior for younger players. See Unity's privacy information at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Profile-picture moderation

If you submit a custom profile picture, the image is sent to Google Cloud Vision API for automated safety analysis before acceptance into the Application's permanent cloud storage. The check may identify adult, sexually suggestive, violent, or other potentially inappropriate material. Images that fail our checks are rejected and are not uploaded to our permanent profile-image storage. Approved images may be stored and shown as part of the relevant player profile, depending on the feature's visibility settings. Automated moderation is not perfect. Google processes the submitted image under the applicable Google Cloud service terms and data-processing commitments. Rejected images may still be processed temporarily by the Application, transmission infrastructure, or the moderation provider; the absence of permanent profile storage does not mean the image was never transmitted or processed.

7. Cloud services, social features and sharing

We use third-party services to operate authentication, cloud saves, content delivery, ads, and platform integrations. Depending on the feature, other players may see nicknames, approved profile images, scores, rankings, or shared levels. We may disclose information to service providers for these purposes, when legally required, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, investigate abuse, and enforce our terms. We do not claim that information sent to third-party services is always anonymized. We do not independently sell player personal information; whether an SDK's advertising-related disclosures are legally considered a “sale” or “sharing” depends on its actual configuration and applicable law.

8. Third-party providers

Services used by Banana Run may include Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication, Cloud Save and Cloud Content Delivery), Unity Ads, Google Play Games Services, Google Play platform services, and Google Cloud Vision API for image moderation. Their processing is subject to applicable service terms and privacy documentation. Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google Cloud: https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice.

9. Retention and security

We retain account and gameplay information while needed to provide the service, and may retain some records afterward when required or permitted for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Storage periods may differ among service providers and backup systems. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service or storage system is completely secure. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in public nicknames, pictures, or custom content.

10. Your choices and rights

You may choose not to upload a profile image, link an external gaming account, or watch a rewarded advertisement. Subject to applicable law, you or your parent/guardian may request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible personal information, and may exercise other applicable privacy rights. To submit a request, email support.lebgames@gmail.com. We may need limited information to verify authority and prevent unauthorized deletion.

11. Account and data deletion

Where available, use the in-game account deletion option or contact support.lebgames@gmail.com. Deleting an account may remove associated cloud progress, profile information, and other linked data according to the implemented deletion process. Some records may remain in provider systems, backups, or legally required retention for a limited period. Uninstalling the Application does not necessarily remove cloud-held data. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

12. International processing

Our cloud and platform providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, applicable safeguards govern international transfers. Consult the relevant provider notices for further information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy as features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted here. Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent before material changes take effect.
